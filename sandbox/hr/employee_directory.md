2. **AI Usage Policy**: The Nexus Internal AI Assistant ("Nexus Assistant") is provided to help employees search internal documentation and IT guides. Employees are prohibited from instructing the bot to reveal confidential financial records or master API keys.
3. **Password Guidelines**: All passwords must be at least 16 characters long and updated every 90 days.
